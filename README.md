# Halftone Listening Room

A 3D shelf of CDs, cassettes and vinyl built with Three.js. Scroll the shelf, drag an album to spin it, click to play.

**Add your own music:** tap "Add your music" (or drop files or a folder onto the page). Albums are grouped by their tags and show the cover art embedded in the files. Everything stays in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

Single static file: `index.html`.
